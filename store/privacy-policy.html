MoneyClarity Calc — privacy policy

Last updated 23 July 2026

MoneyClarity Calc does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no network access of any kind.

Why we can say that so plainly

The app does not request the INTERNET permission. On Android, an application without that permission is prevented by the operating system from opening a network connection. This is not a policy we ask you to take on trust — it is enforced by the platform, and you can verify it yourself: on the Play listing, open About this app and then App permissions, where the entry reads No permissions required.

The app requests no permissions at all. Not contacts, not location, not storage, not the phone state, not the camera.

What stays on your device

Figures you type in are held in memory while you use the app. If you choose to save a calculation, it is written to the app's own private storage area on your phone. That area is readable only by this app.

  • Saved calculations, if you create any.
  • Your light or dark theme preference.
  • Whether you have touch feedback switched on.

None of this is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no copy held elsewhere, because no copy was ever made.

Sharing and exporting

The app can pass a summary of a calculation, or a schedule as a CSV file, to Android's standard share sheet. Nothing is transmitted by the app itself: you choose the destination, and whichever app you pick handles it from there under its own privacy policy.

Links to our website and to the Play listing are handed to your browser or to the Play Store app in the same way, using a standard Android intent. The app never fetches a page itself.

Children

The app is intended for adults. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

What this app is not

MoneyClarity Calc is a calculator. It performs arithmetic on figures you enter. It does not offer credit, arrange or facilitate a loan, introduce you to a lender, sell any financial product, or provide financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Results are estimates for your own information. Figures quoted by an actual provider will govern any real transaction.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new date above. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that would be stated here before the release, not after.
